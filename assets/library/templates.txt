Describe each picture in words (look, clothes, place). Uploading the image itself costs ~1,100 tokens and ~18 s per picture.
Ref order in ComfyUI = Picture order here.

==================== r2v (use system prompt 2a) ====================

--- 1 photo of a person ---
Ref 1: <age, hair, face, clothes>.
Place: <where the video happens, light, time of day>.
<6> s, <one take / N cuts>: <what she does, one continuous action>. Style: <film look / natural / ...>.

--- several photos of the same person ---
Ref 1: <the woman, front face close-up, outfit>. Ref 2: the same woman, <full body / side view, outfit>. Ref 3: the same woman, <...>.
Use the outfit from Ref <N> (or: she wears <new outfit>).
Place: <...>. <6> s, <one take>: <action>. Style: <...>.

--- person + place photo ---
Ref 1: <the person, look, clothes>. Ref 2: <the place, empty, its key features, light>.
<10> s, <2 cuts>: <shot 1 action>, then <shot 2>, then <shot 3>. Style: <...>.

--- one photo gives the person and the place ---
Ref 1: <the woman, look, clothes> in <the place, its features>. Use the place from Ref 1.
<6> s, one take: <action>. Style: <...>.

--- two or more people ---
Ref 1: <person 1, look, clothes>. Ref 2: <person 2, look, clothes>.
Place: <...>. <10> s, <2 cuts>: <who does what, who says what (exact words in quotes)>. Style: <...>.

--- a person without a photo joins the scene ---
Ref 1: <the woman>. Ref 2: <the place>. A <young man with short dark hair, blue shirt> (no photo) <does ...>.
<8> s, one take: <action>. Style: <...>.

--- outfit or prop from another photo ---
Ref 1: <the woman>. Ref 2: <a man wearing a denim jacket with brass buttons>. She wears his jacket.
Place: <...>. <6> s: <action>. Style: <...>.

--- style / mood from a photo ---
Ref 1: <the woman>. Ref 2: <a photo whose look you want: colours, light, grain>. Only copy the look of Ref 2.
Place: <...>. <6> s: <action>.

--- Ref 1 is the exact first frame (turn the First-frame pin group ON in ComfyUI) ---
Ref 1 is the first frame: <what it shows exactly: who, pose, where, framing>.
<6> s, one take: <what happens next>. Style: <...>.

--- with dialogue ---
... <she> says "<exact words>". <he> answers "<exact words>". (Bonsai adds the (S1)/(S2) IDs and <d> tags.)

==================== f2v (use system prompt 2b) ====================

--- first frame only ---
Picture 1: <what the first frame shows: who, clothes, pose, place, light, framing>.
<6> s: <what happens next, one continuous action>. Camera: <pulls back / tracks / static>.

--- first + last frame (turn the Last frame image ON in ComfyUI) ---
Picture 1: <the first frame>. Picture 2: <the last frame>.
<6> s, one shot: <how she gets from the first to the last frame>.

==================== length words ====================
3 s quick test | 6 s one take or 1-2 cuts | 10 s up to 3 cuts | 15 s up to 4 cuts (heavy on the laptop: 0.2-0.3 MP)
